## Step 1

**Project name**

Local402

**Description**

Local402 lets people and their AI agents pay like a local on Celo. Agent services are priced in dollars, but many people and agents hold Argentine pesos, Brazilian reais, Colombian pesos or Nigerian naira, and freelancers and agents in Buenos Aires or Lagos want to be paid in theirs.

It works both ways. Local currency in: anyone holding wARS, wBRL, wCOP or cNGN sees about 145 x402 services (Chainstack, Cencori AI, Google Flights, X, Reddit and more, through buy) priced in their currency, pays in it, and gets the answer. Dollars in: an MCP server lets Claude or any AI assistant hire Celo services, or pay a person or agent exactly what they ask in their own currency.

Textile FX does every currency crossing at a firm quote. Because Textile has a minimum of about $1 per trade, Local402 turns one trade into a payment channel: one Textile fill opens it, each call is a signed EIP-712 voucher (no transaction, no FX), and one fill closes it, refunding the rest in the payer's own currency.

Live on Celo mainnet: 11 Textile fills, 12 paid x402 calls, ERC-8004 agent #10063, every transaction tagged with celo_b049a252d542. Open source (MIT): https://github.com/Nuru-AI/Local402
