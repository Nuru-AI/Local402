**Project name**

Local402

**Description**

Local402 lets anyone, and their AI agent, sip the world's data and AI with no account, no card and no ID, paying in pesos, reais or naira on Celo. One person pays in their own money, and an Unlock code carries that credit to someone who has never had a wallet.

Across Latin America many people are locked out of online services: no card that works abroad, no dollar income, no identity document a sign-up flow will accept. Local402 asks for none of them. A shop, a friend or a community group pays once in Ripio's wARS or in cNGN and splits the credit into Unlock codes. Whoever holds a code can ask what TikTok and Reddit are saying, check a flight, read the chain or ask an AI model, from a simple web call or an AI assistant, one sip at a time. It works the other way too: assistants hire data and AI on Celo and pay the people and agents who work for them in their own currency. The agent itself carries a Self Agent ID, so anyone can check that a real, verified person stands behind it, while the people it serves never have to show one.

Every currency crossing runs through Textile FX at a firm quote. A Textile trade starts at about one dollar, far more than one sip, so Local402 makes each trade open a payment channel. It is the same design behind MPP sessions, x402 batch settlement and Apify's one-dollar prepaid token, all of which we already run in production. One Textile fill opens the channel, every call is a signed voucher with no transaction and no exchange, and one fill closes it, returning the rest in the buyer's own money.

The data and AI come through buy, Celo's marketplace for agents: X and Reddit search, TikTok data from Apify, Celo RPC from Chainstack and answers from Cencori, each paid per call over x402. Live on Celo mainnet: 12 Textile fills, 15 paid calls, Unlock codes spent with no wallet, ERC-8004 agent #10063 with Self Agent ID #198, every transaction tagged. Built by the team behind Sippar. Open source: https://github.com/Nuru-AI/Local402

Building prosperity for all, one sip at a time.
