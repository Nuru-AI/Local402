How it works

  1. 1
    Pay in your own moneyPay once on Celo in Ripio's wARS or in cNGN.
  2. 2
    One Textile trade opens a channelTextile FX converts the payment at a firm quote. A trade starts at about one dollar, so one trade opens a payment channel instead of paying for a single call.
  3. 3
    Sip data and AI with signed vouchersEach call is a signed voucher, with no transaction and no exchange. Local402 pays the service over x402 through buy.
  4. 4
    Close and get the rest backOne more Textile trade returns what is left in the buyer's own currency.

It works the other way too. Connect the Local402 MCP server and an AI assistant can hire data and AI on Celo, or buy a budget in pesos or naira in one Textile trade and pay people and agents from it, one signed voucher at a time, within a spending cap you agreed to.

The easy way in: an Unlock code

An Unlock code holds credit for data and AI, so the person using it needs no wallet, account or ID.

Pay in pesos or naira on Celo and the credit can be split into codes. Give one to someone over WhatsApp or on paper and it works the same for them, from a simple web call or an AI assistant.

  • Card checkout, like Sippar's Unlock card, is next.
  • Most calls above draw half a cent to a cent from a code.
  • Codes buy data and AI through Local402 and cannot be cashed out.
