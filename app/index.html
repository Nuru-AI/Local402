How it works

  1. Pay in your own money. Someone holding wARS (Ripio), wBRL, wCOP or cNGN pays once on Celo. A shop, a friend or a community group can split that credit into Unlock codes for people with no wallet at all.
  2. One Textile trade opens a channel. Textile FX converts the payment at a firm quote. Its trades start at about one dollar, so one trade opens a payment channel instead of paying for a single call.
  3. Sip data and AI. Every call is a signed voucher, with no transaction and no exchange: X, Reddit and TikTok data (via Apify), Celo RPC from Chainstack, answers from Cencori, all bought through buy over x402.
  4. Close. One more Textile trade returns what is left in the buyer's own currency.

It works the other way too: through the Local402 MCP server, Claude and other assistants hire data and AI on Celo and pay people and agents in their own currency.
